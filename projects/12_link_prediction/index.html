Link Prediction in Complex Networks Using Community Structure Information

Researcher (M.Sc. project) · 2011–2013

Master’s research aimed at developing link prediction methods for complex networks based on structural and community information. The research studied how clustering properties and community organization could improve the estimation of future relationships in social networks.

Project details

  • Role: Researcher
  • Supervisor: Prof. Alneu de Andrade Lopes
  • Institution: Universidade de São Paulo (USP), Brazil
  • Period: 2011–2013
  • Funding: CNPq

Results

  • Development and evaluation of link prediction methods based on community information and cluster structure.
  • Application of the methods to online social networks, including Twitter.
