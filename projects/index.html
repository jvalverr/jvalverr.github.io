Projects

Applied AI case studies in Large Language Models, healthcare, education, enterprise systems and government, connecting rigorous research with real-world implementation in Latin America.

ONGOING LLM SECURITY PROMPT INJECTION GUARDRAILS ADVERSARIAL ROBUSTNESS

Research on security, robustness and adversarial threats in Large Language Model systems, with a focus on prompt injection, guardrails and reliable deployment of LLM-based applications.

AI Assurance for Aviation and Safety-Critical Systems

ONGOING SAFETY-CRITICAL AI AI ASSURANCE HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP DECISION READINESS

Research on trustworthy and reliable AI for aviation and safety-critical systems, with a focus on decision assurance, human oversight and the operational validation of Generative AI outputs.

Research project developed by Tecnológico de Monterrey and the Cybersecurity Agency of El Salvador, focused on applied research in cyber defense and on countering disinformation operations.
