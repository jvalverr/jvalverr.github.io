I work across the full lifecycle of **Artificial Intelligence systems**, from research and experimental design to architecture, evaluation, deployment and organizational adoption.

My technical expertise is centered on **Large Language Models, Generative AI, Natural Language Processing, machine learning, AI agents, model evaluation, privacy, security and responsible AI**, with a strong focus on translating these technologies into reliable solutions for complex real-world environments.

My work spans **business, education, healthcare and government**, combining scientific rigor with practical constraints such as data quality, infrastructure, scalability, privacy, governance and measurable impact across Latin America.

## Research, technology and real-world impact

Over the years, I have worked at the intersection of **applied research, technical leadership and AI product development**, leading and collaborating with multidisciplinary teams on systems designed for high-impact environments.

My experience includes **clinical AI systems, enterprise AI applications, privacy-preserving LLM architectures, intelligent automation, data analytics platforms and AI solutions for public-sector and educational contexts**.

A central part of my work is evaluating whether AI systems remain reliable outside controlled experimental settings — under ambiguity, domain-specific constraints, limited data, security requirements and real organizational processes.

## Technical expertise

My current technical work focuses on:

- Large Language Models and Generative AI architectures
- LLM evaluation, reasoning, robustness and benchmarking
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation and knowledge-grounded systems
- AI agents and multi-agent workflows
- Fine-tuning, adaptation and efficient model deployment
- AI security, prompt injection and adversarial robustness
- Privacy-preserving AI and responsible AI governance
- Natural Language Processing and multilingual AI
- Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Data Analytics
- Applied AI systems for business, education and government

## Education, mentoring and knowledge transfer

Alongside research and technology development, I am deeply involved in **higher education, executive education and professional mentoring**.

I teach and supervise applied projects in Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI and Data Analytics, including graduate and MBA-level work, and contribute to continuing-education programs designed for professionals and decision-makers.

This experience has reinforced one of the principles behind my work: advanced AI creates more value when technical knowledge can be translated into **clear decisions, reproducible methods and capabilities that organizations can actually sustain**.

## Bridging research and implementation

I am particularly interested in problems where model performance alone is not enough.

Successful AI deployment also requires understanding **data pipelines, system architecture, evaluation methodology, privacy, security, human oversight, organizational processes and business or institutional impact**.

This perspective shapes both my research and my work with organizations: moving beyond promising prototypes toward AI systems that are **technically sound, responsible, measurable and scalable**.
