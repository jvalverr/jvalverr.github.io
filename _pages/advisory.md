<p class="lead">
  <strong>Helping organizations move from AI experimentation to technically sound, responsible and scalable implementation.</strong>
</p>

I work with organizations, research groups and institutional leaders facing complex decisions around **Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models and Generative AI**.

My approach combines **technical depth, applied research and strategic decision-making**, helping teams evaluate what should be built, how it should be validated, which risks need to be addressed and what is required to move an AI initiative into a real operational environment.

I am particularly interested in problems where model performance alone is not enough — and where architecture, evaluation, privacy, security, governance, infrastructure and organizational adoption are equally important.

## AI Strategy and Adoption

Strategic support for organizations defining where and how Artificial Intelligence can create meaningful and sustainable value.

Typical engagements include:

- AI opportunity and readiness assessment.
- Strategic roadmaps and initiative prioritization.
- Build, buy or partner decisions.
- Technical and organizational feasibility analysis.
- AI architecture and technology selection.
- Proof-of-concept to production planning.
- Evaluation of existing AI initiatives and technical roadmaps.

## LLM and Generative AI Systems

Technical design and evaluation of systems built around Large Language Models and Generative AI.

Typical engagements include:

- LLM system architecture.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation and knowledge-grounded systems.
- Fine-tuning and model adaptation strategies.
- LLM evaluation, benchmarking and failure analysis.
- Agentic and multi-agent architectures.
- Model, framework and infrastructure selection.
- Production-oriented prototypes.
- Cost, latency and scalability analysis.
- Human-in-the-loop and hybrid AI workflows.

The focus is not only on whether a model works, but on whether the **complete system is reliable, measurable and operationally viable**.

## AI Evaluation, Security and Responsible Deployment

Independent technical assessment of AI systems operating in complex or high-impact environments.

Areas of work include:

- Model and system evaluation.
- Robustness and reliability testing.
- Prompt-injection and adversarial analysis.
- Privacy-preserving AI architectures.
- Data governance and information protection.
- Human oversight and traceability.
- Evaluation under domain-specific and multilingual conditions.
- Responsible AI guidelines and deployment criteria.
- Monitoring and post-deployment evaluation strategies.

This work is especially relevant when AI systems interact with sensitive data, institutional processes or decisions with significant human impact.

## Applied Research and Technical Partnerships

Collaboration with organizations and research teams seeking to explore technically challenging AI problems with scientific rigor.

Possible engagements include:

- Applied AI research projects.
- Experimental design and validation.
- Benchmark and evaluation protocol development.
- LLM and Generative AI research.
- AI security and privacy research.
- Multilingual and culturally grounded AI.
- Research–industry collaboration.
- Technical review of AI methodologies and systems.
- Scientific publications and technology-transfer initiatives.

These collaborations are designed to connect **research questions with real-world constraints**, producing evidence that can support both scientific advancement and practical implementation.
