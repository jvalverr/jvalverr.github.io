Research, development and innovation program aimed at building an intelligent platform for analyzing professional skills, matching people with job opportunities, and supporting recruitment and internal mobility processes. The solution integrated NLP, semantic representation of skills, recommender systems, analytics and predictive models. The project evolved from research funded by FAPESP/PIPE to a commercial launch and technology transfer to Petrobras.

### Project details

- **Role:** Principal Investigator / Responsible Researcher
- **Institutions:** Visibilia / FAPESP / Petrobras, Brazil
- **Period:** 2017–2023
- **Funding:**
  - FAPESP PIPE Phase 1 – Process 2016/08183-5
  - FAPESP PIPE Phase 2 – Process 2019/02455-1
  - BeeNet Petro – collaboration with Petrobras, 2022–2023

### Results

- Development and validation of skill-based job recommendation algorithms.
- Development of models and taxonomies for representing professional skills.
- Development, technical validation and commercial launch of BeeNet.
- User testing and product evolution for operation at scale.
- Customer acquisition through B2B and B2C models between 2021 and 2022.
- Software registration at INPI (Brazilian National Institute of Industrial Property).
- Winner of an international challenge promoted by Petrobras in 2022.
- Acquisition of BeeNet by Petrobras in 2023 and technology transfer of the solution for use in a large-scale corporate environment.
