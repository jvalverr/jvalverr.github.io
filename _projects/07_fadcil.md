Deep Learning system developed for the automated detection of COVID-19 and the quantification of lung lesions in computed tomography (CT) scans. The solution integrated detection and segmentation models for medical images, inference APIs, computing infrastructure and traceability mechanisms for use in clinical environments.

### Project details

- **Role:** Principal Investigator / Responsible Researcher
- **Institutions:** Visibilia / Hospital das Clínicas da Faculdade de Medicina da Universidade de São Paulo (HCFMUSP), Brazil
- **Period:** 2020–2023

### Results

- Winner of an innovation challenge promoted by HCFMUSP and the São Paulo State Government in 2020.
- Development and validation of Deep Learning models for the detection and segmentation of lung lesions in CT scans.
- Registration of the FADCIL software at INPI (Brazilian National Institute of Industrial Property).
- Acquisition of FADCIL by HCFMUSP, enabling its technology transfer and application in a real clinical setting.
