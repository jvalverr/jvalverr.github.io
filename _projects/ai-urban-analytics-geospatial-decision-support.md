### Research outputs

- A self-supervised urban representation learning framework that identifies
  unusual crime-reporting patterns relative to representation-similar urban
  regions.
- **Neighbor Profile Divergence (NPD):** a measure of how far a region's
  reporting profile deviates from the behavior expected from its peers,
  evaluated on more than 1.8 million crime records from Mexico City.
