Applied research project on the adaptation and evaluation of Large Language Models for government and legal contexts involving sensitive data. The research covers privacy, teacher–student learning, knowledge distillation, efficient fine-tuning, LoRA, quantization, model evaluation, factuality, and the reduction of errors and hallucinations.

### Project details

- **Role:** Collaborating Researcher
- **Principal Investigator:** Ricardo Marcacini
- **Institutions:** Universidade de São Paulo (USP) / Procuradoria-Geral da Fazenda Nacional (PGFN), Brazil
- **Period:** 2024–2026

### Results

- Development and evaluation of privacy-preserving strategies for specializing LLMs in sensitive domains.
- Evaluation of models for the classification and analysis of legal documents.
- Development of teacher–student strategies combined with distillation, LoRA and quantization.
- Best Paper Award at LASDiGov 2025.
