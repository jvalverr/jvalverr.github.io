Doctoral project focused on the study of social, structural and mobility information in location-based social networks, with an emphasis on modeling user behavior and predicting relationships.

### Project details

- **Role:** Researcher
- **Supervisor:** Prof. Alneu de Andrade Lopes
- **Institution:** Universidade de São Paulo (USP), Brazil
- **Period:** 2014–2017
- **Funding:** FAPESP

### Results

- Development of relationship prediction models that integrate information on social structure, location and user mobility patterns.
