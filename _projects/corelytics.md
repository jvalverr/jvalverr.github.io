### Overview

Corelytics is a research and development project focused on the use of
**Generative AI, Large Language Models and intelligent agents to support Data
Analytics and Data Science projects**.

The first stage investigated how advanced language models, visualization tools
and interactive computing environments could help students without extensive
programming experience develop analytical solutions while preserving the
learning processes required to understand data, methods and results.

The project has since evolved toward an **agent-based architecture** in which
specialized intelligent agents support different stages of the analytics
lifecycle, including problem formulation, data preparation, exploratory
analysis, modeling, interpretation and communication of results.

The broader objective is to investigate how Generative AI can assist analytical
work without reducing Data Analytics to automatic code generation, supporting
instead the development of stronger analytical reasoning and computational
thinking.

### Research questions

The project explores questions such as:

- How can Generative AI support users with limited programming experience
  throughout a Data Analytics project?
- How can intelligent agents provide specialized support across different
  stages of the analytics lifecycle?
- How should AI-assisted analytical environments balance automation with
  learning and human reasoning?
- How can Large Language Models, visualization tools and interactive computing
  environments be integrated into coherent analytical workflows?
- How can agent-based systems evaluate intermediate results and provide
  context-aware guidance during Data Science projects?
- What forms of AI assistance improve analytical productivity without weakening
  the development of core Data Analytics skills?

### Current research direction

The project currently focuses on the evolution of Corelytics toward a
**multi-agent architecture for supporting end-to-end Data Analytics and Data
Science workflows**.

The research is expanding toward **specialized agents, adaptive guidance,
workflow orchestration and evaluation of human-AI collaboration in analytical
projects**.

### Research outputs

- **Corelytics platform:** an AI-assisted environment designed to support Data
  Analytics and Data Science projects through Large Language Models,
  visualization tools and intelligent agents.
- Integration of Generative AI with interactive analytical and visualization
  environments.
- Evaluation of AI-assisted Data Analytics learning among students without
  advanced programming backgrounds.
- Evolution from LLM-based assistance toward specialized agent-based support
  across the analytics lifecycle.
