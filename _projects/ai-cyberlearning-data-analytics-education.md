### Project details

- **Role:** Research Collaborator
- **Institution:** Tecnológico de Monterrey
- **Period:** 2024–Present

### Overview

This research line investigates how **Artificial Intelligence, Learning
Analytics and data-driven methods can support teaching, learning and
educational decision-making in higher education**.

The work combines educational research with Data Science and Artificial
Intelligence to study student success, academic risk, learning models,
educational technology and the use of AI-based tools in learning environments.

A central objective is to move beyond the use of AI merely as an automation
tool and investigate how data and intelligent technologies can help educators
and institutions better understand **how students learn, which factors influence
academic outcomes and how educational interventions can be designed more
effectively**.

The project also explores how Generative AI and interactive technologies can
be integrated into educational experiences while preserving meaningful
learning, human judgment and pedagogical objectives.

### Research questions

The project explores questions such as:

- How can Learning Analytics help identify patterns associated with student
  success and academic risk?
- Which demographic, academic and co-curricular factors influence educational
  outcomes?
- How can Artificial Intelligence support teaching and learning without
  replacing the cognitive processes required for meaningful learning?
- How do students interact with AI-assisted educational environments compared
  with traditional learning models?
- How can educational data support more inclusive and evidence-based
  interventions?
- How can Generative AI and interactive technologies improve learning in STEM
  and other higher-education contexts?
- How can predictive analytics support early identification of students at
  risk of academic failure?

### Current research direction

The project currently focuses on **Learning Analytics, student success,
AI-assisted education and the evaluation of emerging learning technologies in
higher education**.

The research is expanding toward **predictive educational analytics, adaptive
learning environments, Generative AI in teaching and evidence-based models for
supporting student learning and academic success**.

### Research outputs

- Predictive Learning Analytics models for identifying patterns associated
  with academic success and risk of failure across different academic
  disciplines.
- Data-driven studies of demographic, socioeconomic and co-curricular factors
  influencing student outcomes.
- Evaluation of Generative AI and AI chatbots as educational support tools.
- Analysis of challenge-based learning models and their impact on student
  performance and competency development.
- Investigation of visuo-haptic and AI-assisted learning experiences in
  introductory STEM education.
- Learning Analytics studies supporting a broader understanding of academic
  success beyond traditional indicators such as GPA.

### Research artifacts

- Predictive Learning Analytics models for student success and academic risk.
- Educational datasets covering academic, demographic and co-curricular
  variables.
- Analytical frameworks for evaluating student learning and educational
  interventions.
- AI-assisted learning activities combining Generative AI and interactive
  educational technologies.
