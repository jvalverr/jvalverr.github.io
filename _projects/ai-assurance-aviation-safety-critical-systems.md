### Project details

- **Role:** Research Collaborator
- **Institutions:** George Mason University (USA), Tecnologico de Monterrey (Mexico), Department of Airspace Control — DECEA (Brazil)
- **Period:** 2026–2028

### Overview

Generative AI and Large Language Models are increasingly being considered for
decision-support tasks in domains where errors can have significant operational
or safety consequences.

Aviation provides a particularly demanding environment for this research.
Activities such as flight planning require AI-generated recommendations to
remain consistent, interpretable and compliant with strict operational and
regulatory constraints. A fluent or apparently plausible response is not
necessarily a safe or operationally valid one.

This project investigates **AI assurance mechanisms for aviation and other
safety-critical systems**, focusing on how probabilistic AI outputs can be
evaluated before they influence operational decisions.

The broader objective is to develop methods that bridge the gap between the
flexibility of Generative AI and the deterministic reliability requirements of
high-stakes environments.

### Research questions

The project explores questions such as:

- How can the reliability of AI-generated recommendations be assessed before operational use?
- How can semantic stability and operational consistency be measured across equivalent AI interactions?
- How can domain-specific safety and regulatory constraints be incorporated into AI evaluation?
- How should uncertainty influence the level of authority granted to an AI-generated recommendation?
- How can human oversight be calibrated according to the reliability of AI outputs?
- How can assurance mechanisms remain model-independent and transferable across safety-critical domains?

### Current research direction

The project currently focuses on **decision assurance, operational consistency
and constraint-aware evaluation of Generative AI in aviation**.

The research is expanding toward **adaptive assurance mechanisms, adversarial
robustness, longitudinal reliability and validation with real operational data
across safety-critical environments**.

### Research outputs

- **ATAL — AI Trust and Assurance Layer:** a model-agnostic framework for
  evaluating whether AI-generated outputs are sufficiently stable, operationally
  consistent and compliant with domain constraints to support high-stakes
  decision-making.

### Research artifacts

- AI Trust and Assurance Layer (ATAL).
- Semantic Stability Score (SSS).
- Operational Consistency Score (OCS).
- Normative Constraint Validation (NCV).
- Decision Readiness Level (DRL).
