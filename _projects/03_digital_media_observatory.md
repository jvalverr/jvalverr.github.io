Interdisciplinary project developed with the Digital Media Observatory of Tecnológico de Monterrey to study digital media, public opinion and disinformation phenomena through Data Analytics, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Machine Learning and Large Language Models. It includes building infrastructure to collect and analyze data from different social platforms, and creating multimodal, multi-platform corpora.

### Project details

- **Role:** Collaborating Researcher
- **Principal Investigator:** Martín Alejandro del Campo Huerta
- **Institution:** Tecnológico de Monterrey
- **Period:** 2024–2026
- **Funding:** Challenge-Based Research Funding Program

### Results

- Development and deployment of a web system for large-scale, multi-platform collection of social media data, with integration, storage, exploration and visualization through Apache Superset and complementary Data Engineering and Analytics technologies.
- Construction of corpora on political discourse and social phenomena in Spanish.
- Development of experimental protocols for the analysis of sentiment, emotions, humor, sarcasm, stance and cultural reasoning.
- Comparison of traditional NLP methods, pre-trained transformers and Large Language Models.
- 3rd Best Paper Award at MICAI 2025.
