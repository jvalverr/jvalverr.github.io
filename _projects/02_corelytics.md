Research and development line on the use of Generative AI and intelligent agents to support Data Analytics and Data Science projects. The first stage investigated the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) and visualization tools to enable students without advanced programming experience to build analytical solutions. The second stage evolves Corelytics toward an architecture based on specialized agents that support the different phases of an analytics project lifecycle.

### Project details

- **Role:** Principal Investigator
- **Institution:** Tecnológico de Monterrey – Institute for the Future of Education (IFE)
- **Period:** 2024–2027
- **Funding:** NOVUS 2023 (ID 419283); NOVUS 2026 (ID 151098)

### Results

- Design and development of the Corelytics platform to support Data Analytics and Data Science projects.
- Integration of Large Language Models, visualization tools and interactive computing environments.
- Evaluation of the use of generative AI by students without advanced programming training.
- Evolution of the platform toward an intelligent agent-based architecture to support analytics projects.
- Software registration in progress.
